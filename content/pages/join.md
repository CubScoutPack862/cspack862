## A family adventure

Cub Scouting is designed for families. Parents and guardians participate alongside Scouts and help create a welcoming experience.

## Becoming a registered volunteer
Help coordinate the event assigned to your Den, support popcorn sales and church fundraisers when needed, or lend a hand at meetings, camping, Pinewood Derby, Bridging, the Holiday Party, and the End of Year Picnic.

1. Complete the [Pack 862 adult volunteer application (external)](pack:adult).
2. Complete [Safeguarding Youth Training (external)](pack:safeguarding) annually, following the Pack checklist.
3. Complete the current [California / GLAAC compliance requirements (external)](pack:california), including online training, LiveScan, and the Background Consent Form.
4. Follow the Pack’s direction for position-specific training through [My.Scouting (external)](pack:training) and the adult leader uniform.
