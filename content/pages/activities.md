## Camping and outdoor outings

Camping is a Pack tradition. This season’s tentative plan includes Boo Fest Camping and Rocket Academy Camping. Find the dates on our [Pack calendar](/calendar/). 

## Pinewood Derby & Raingutter Regatta

Build a car, prepare for race day, and cheer on your fellow Scouts. Our calendar includes Pinewood Derby car cutting, setup and Pack pictures, and the Derby itself. Raingutter Regatta brings another hands-on racing tradition to the Pack. Dues include the Derby car and Regatta boat.

## Celebrations and milestones

The Holiday Party, Blue & Gold Party, Game Night, and End of Year Picnic bring families together. Bridging celebrates Scouts’ next steps, including older Cub Scouts’ transition toward a Scout troop.

## Service and citizenship

 The Pack participates in the annual Flag-raising Ceremony at Roosevelt Memorial Park. Service and conservation also feature in rank adventures.
