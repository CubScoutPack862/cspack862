## Getting started

1. **Explore Pack 862.** Learn about our [grade-based dens](/about/#dens), [activities](/activities/), and [tentative calendar](/calendar/).
2. **Review the season cost.** The approved {{season}} baseline is {{totalFee}}: {{youthFee}} for the National/Council fee plus {{packDues}} in Pack dues, due {{duesDate}}.
3. **Register your Scout.** Use the [Pack 862 youth application on Scouting America (external)](pack:youth).
4. **Review safeguarding and health-form guidance.** Use the official links below. Do not send health information through this website.
5. **Prepare for the first meeting.** Review uniform guidance and [email the Pack](/contact/) with general questions.

Additional registered-family details are provided privately after registration.

## Uniforms

Uniform pieces vary by rank. Review the [official Cub Scout uniform guidance (external)](pack:uniform) before shopping. Pack 862 serves:

- Lion — Kindergarten
- Tiger — 1st grade
- Wolf — 2nd grade
- Bear — 3rd grade
- Webelos — 4th grade
- Arrow of Light — 5th grade
