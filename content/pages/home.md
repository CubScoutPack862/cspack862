## A place to belong. Room to grow.

Welcome to Pack 862. We bring Scouts and families together through outdoor adventures, camping, Pinewood Derby, celebrations, and opportunities to learn and grow.

Faith United Methodist Church is our charter organization and primary meeting location. Parents and guardians help make this family program possible—one adventure at a time.
